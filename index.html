Privacy Policy

HAVRYNN Regnskap · Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains how HAVRYNN Regnskap handles information. It does not ask you to waive privacy rights or constitute consent to optional processing.

1. Publisher and contact

The publisher is Robert Andreassen. For privacy questions, contact r.andreassen@live.no.

2. Records you enter

The app helps you organise receipts, expenses, mileage claims, reimbursements, imported bank transactions and working time. Information you enter may include names, dates, amounts, suppliers, work descriptions, payment references, images and other attachments. The app uses these details for record keeping, reconciliation, history and reports.

You or the organisation using the app decide what information to enter, who may use the app, and how records are used. If you enter information about other people, make sure you are authorised to do so and provide any notices required for your use of the app. This statement does not exclude any responsibility the publisher may have under applicable law.

The app stores its accounting database and attachments in the app's private storage on the device. The live database and attachments are not separately encrypted by the app. Android device security, including a screen lock, remains important. Local user accounts, names, roles and salted password hashes stay on that device; they are not online accounts with the publisher. Work-time records may contain the name of the person who worked.

3. Optional organisation sync with Cloudflare

Organisation sync is optional. It starts only after an administrator creates or joins an organisation, or a user accepts an invitation. When sync is enabled, the app encrypts accounting events and receipt contents on the device with AES-GCM before sending them over HTTPS to a Cloudflare Worker and Cloudflare D1 database. The organisation key is protected on Android with Android Keystore. It is shared with an invited device using an encrypted envelope derived from the one-time invitation code. The publisher does not receive the organisation key in plaintext through this process.

Encrypted event contents can include accounting entries, work-time records, mileage settings, bank transactions, reimbursements and receipt attachments. The app currently synchronises these records only between Android installations. Windows installations are not yet part of organisation sync.

Cloudflare must process some information to authenticate members and route the event log. This includes the organisation name, member display names and roles, hashed access tokens and invitation codes, event and entity identifiers, event types, revision numbers, sequence numbers and timestamps, as well as encrypted event payloads. Cloudflare also processes technical request information needed to provide its service, which may include network and security data such as IP addresses. The current app does not send local account passwords to Cloudflare.

This is central cloud storage, not a direct phone-to-phone transfer and not a separate backup. Cloudflare's infrastructure may process information under Cloudflare's terms and privacy notices. D1 provides provider-managed encryption at rest and TLS for transfers within Cloudflare; this is separate from the app's encryption of event contents. Cloudflare can still see the unencrypted membership and event-routing metadata described above. See Cloudflare D1 data security and Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

The event log is append-only. The app currently does not provide a control to erase an organisation's cloud event history. Removing a member prevents that member's future API access but cannot delete records already downloaded to their device. Clearing or uninstalling the app removes local app data but does not delete the Cloudflare organisation or its event log. Do not rely on sync as your only recovery copy. Ask the organisation administrator about access, retention and cloud data removal before adding records.

4. Invitations

An administrator creates a one-time invitation code and shares it with the intended person. Anyone who obtains the code before it expires or is accepted may be able to join with the assigned role. Share codes securely and revoke unused codes if they may have been exposed. Local login passwords are not transferred with an invitation; each device has its own local account.

5. Receipt recognition and Google ML Kit

Receipt recognition uses Google ML Kit. Images and recognised text are processed on the device. Google states that the image and recognised text are not sent to Google servers for this processing.

The SDK may send technical diagnostics and usage information to Google, including device model and operating system, app and SDK versions, installation identifiers, performance measurements, errors, feature events and input formats and sizes. Google states that this transmission is encrypted using HTTPS. The app contains no advertising.

See ML Kit Terms and Privacy, ML Kit data disclosures and Google's Privacy Policy. Third-party terms do not remove the publisher's obligations concerning the SDK used in this app.

6. Optional file backups

You may create a separate backup and choose a destination through Android's document picker, such as Google Drive or OneDrive. A connected backup file may be updated after changes, including by background work. The backup contains the database, local user accounts, history and attachments and is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using your backup password. Android Keystore protects the password stored locally for automatic backup. Your chosen provider handles its own account, storage and file transfer. Disconnecting stops future updates but does not delete existing files. The publisher cannot recover a forgotten backup password.

7. Reports, sharing and permissions

PDF and Excel reports are saved where you choose. They are not password-encrypted by the app and may contain personal or financial information and receipt images. Review recipients and file contents before sharing. The publisher does not sell app records.

The app uses Android's document picker and the camera app for files or receipt photographs you select. Monthly reminders are optional and require notification permission on newer Android versions. The app does not request access to contacts, SMS or location.

8. Support and this website

If you contact the publisher, your email address, message and any information you provide are used to respond, investigate problems or handle legal requests. Do not send passwords, full backups or unnecessary financial or sensitive personal information. Redact examples before sending them. Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed for the enquiry, applicable legal obligations or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

The public privacy and terms pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, under its Privacy Statement. These pages add no analytics or advertising scripts. Cloudflare, GitHub, Google services and a backup provider may process information outside Norway or the EEA; consult their notices for details about processing locations and transfer safeguards. This notice does not promise that all processing takes place in Norway or the EEA.

9. Retention, deletion and rights

Local records remain on the device until you or an administrator delete them, clear app storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Audit history and records linked to completed reimbursements may be protected against individual deletion to preserve consistency. Deactivating a local user does not erase historical entries. Exports and backup files must be deleted separately at their chosen locations.

The organisation using the app may control the purposes and access rules for its accounting records. Contact that organisation's administrator about its records and retention decisions. Contact Robert Andreassen at r.andreassen@live.no about the publisher's processing. Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Rights may be subject to lawful exceptions, including record-retention requirements. You may complain to Datatilsynet or another competent supervisory authority.
