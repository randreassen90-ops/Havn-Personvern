Privacy Policy

HAVRYNN Regnskap · Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains how HAVRYNN Regnskap handles information. It does not ask you to waive privacy rights or constitute consent to optional processing.

1. Publisher and contact

The publisher is Robert Andreassen. For privacy questions, contact r.andreassen@live.no.

2. Records you enter

The app helps you organise receipts, expenses, mileage claims, reimbursements, imported bank records and time entries. These may include names, dates, amounts, suppliers, work descriptions, images and other attachments you choose. They are used for record keeping, reconciliation, history and reports.

Records and attachments are stored in the app's private storage on your device. The publisher does not operate an app server receiving these records. Optional local user profiles contain names, roles and salted password hashes; they are not online accounts with the publisher. The app does not separately encrypt the live database or attachments. Use device security such as a screen lock.

If an organisation uses the app to process other people's information, it is responsible for its own purposes, lawful basis, access permissions, notices and retention decisions. This does not exclude any responsibility the publisher has under applicable law.

3. Receipt recognition and Google ML Kit

Receipt recognition uses Google ML Kit. Images and recognised text are processed on the device; according to Google, those inputs and recognition outputs are not sent to Google servers for this processing.

The SDK may send technical diagnostics and usage information to Google, including device model and operating system, app and SDK versions, installation identifiers, performance measurements, error codes, feature events, and input formats and sizes. Google states that this information is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. The app contains no advertising.

See ML Kit Terms and Privacy, ML Kit data disclosures and Google's Privacy Policy. A reference to a third party does not remove the publisher's obligations concerning the SDK used in this app.

4. Optional backups and cloud storage

You choose whether to create a backup and where to store it through Android's document picker. If you connect cloud storage, the selected backup file is updated after changes, including through background work. Backups contain the database, local profiles, history and attachments. They are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using your backup password. Android Keystore protects the locally stored password used for automatic backup.

Your chosen provider, such as Google Drive or OneDrive, handles its own account, storage and transfers. Disconnecting cloud storage stops future synchronisation; it does not delete existing backup files. Keep your backup password safe: the publisher has no password-recovery service for these files.

5. Exports and sharing

PDF and Excel reports are saved where you choose and are not password-encrypted by the app. They may include personal information and receipt images. Information leaves the device if you choose a cloud destination or share files yourself. Review recipients and file contents before sharing. The publisher does not sell your app records.

6. Permissions

The app uses Android's document picker and the camera app for files or receipt photographs you select. Monthly reminders are optional and require notification permission on newer Android versions. The app does not request access to contacts, SMS or location.

7. Support communications and this website

If you contact the publisher, your email address, message and any information you send are processed to respond, investigate problems or handle legal requests. Do not send passwords, full backups or unnecessary financial or sensitive personal information. Redact examples before sending them.

For support correspondence, the publisher relies on the legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and maintaining the app, subject to your interests and rights (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)); where necessary to meet a legal obligation, Article 6(1)(c) applies. Correspondence is retained only as long as necessary for that purpose, including applicable legal obligations or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. You may contact the publisher about retention or deletion.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical request information, which may include your IP address, under its Privacy Statement. These pages add no analytics, advertising scripts or cookies. Email, hosting, Google services and your chosen cloud provider may process information outside the EEA; their privacy notices describe locations and applicable transfer safeguards. This policy does not promise that all processing takes place in Norway or the EEA.

8. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you or your administrator delete them, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Audit history and records linked to completed reimbursements may be protected against individual deletion to preserve consistency. Deactivating a local user does not erase historical entries.

To remove all local data, clear the app's storage in Android settings. First preserve records you are required to retain. Exports and backup files must be deleted separately at their storage locations. The publisher cannot remotely erase or recover your local files. Retention of Google's SDK diagnostics is governed by Google's practices and applicable law.

9. Your rights

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Rights may be subject to lawful exceptions, including record-retention obligations. Contact the publisher using the email above; for records controlled by an organisation using the app, also contact that organisation.

You may complain to Datatilsynet or another competent supervisory authority. The publisher does not use app records to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

The date above identifies this version. Material changes to processing will be communicated as required by law; publishing a revised policy does not itself obtain consent.
